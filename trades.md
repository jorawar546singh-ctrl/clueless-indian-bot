Every closed trade gets an entry. No exceptions. The journal is the discipline; the dashboard is just the tool.

## Position sizing rule (locked, non-adjustable)

**2% of account = max loss per trade.**

Formula (run this BEFORE every entry):

```
max_shares = floor( (account_size * 0.02) / (entry_price - stop_price) )
```

At ₹25,000 account: max loss = ₹500. If you risk more, you violated the rule.

One position at a time at current account size. Period.
